Parking

Please use the main parking lot in front of the shopping center first. If the main lot is full, additional parking is available in the overflow lot off Whiteford Place. On-street parking is also permitted in the residential streets behind the shopping center; be respectful of driveways and neighbors.

  • Main lot Primary lot at the center — closest to The Toy Outpost.
  • Overflow (Whiteford Place) If the main lot is full, follow signage or use the lot accessible from Whiteford Place.
  • Residential street parking Streets behind the center allow parking where posted; watch for signs and leave driveways clear.
